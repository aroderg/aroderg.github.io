Nox Tower Defense

A solo-tower incremental tower defense game where your entire defense strategy revolves around optimizing and evolving a single tower. Features unlockable active skills, deep upgrade trees, dynamic enemy waves, and idle progression elements. Built in Lua with LÖVE2D.

Project: Cooldown

An incremental timing game inspired by classic waiting-style games. Players activate actions, unlock cooldown modifiers, and accumulate passive power over time, allowing progress to continue smoothly even during offline play. Built using Lua & LÖVE2D.
